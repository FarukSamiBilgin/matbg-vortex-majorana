# Extended abstract

**Can a vortex in magic-angle graphene host a Majorana zero mode? A geometric obstruction, and the measurement that settles it**

Faruk Sami Bilgin, Independent Researcher (ORCID 0009-0001-9064-4410)

**Status: Manuscript in preparation.** The full draft is not included in this repository.

**Claim class.** Analytic desk calculation on published literature numbers, plus Pfaffian (Chern-parity) evaluations on a minimal lattice model and on Bistritzer–MacDonald continuum bands. Not a vortex Bogoliubov–de Gennes solve, not a transport simulation, not a measurement.

The usual argument for "no" (nodal gap, therefore no Caroli–de Gennes–Matricon minigap, therefore no protected zero mode) is not valid: the *parity* of the Chern number remains defined in a gapless superconductor and can protect a Majorana in a vortex of odd vorticity. The author had made the invalid argument in his own decision log and withdraws it explicitly.

The obstruction that does bind is geometric. In a gate-defined mesa the fluxoid condition makes the Little–Parks step `Φ₀/A_mesa`, so the vortex-placement field and the width of the window that counts vortices are the same number. At the 2.76 mT working field of the design, three estimates put the Volovik zero-energy density of states at 5–26 % of normal, against the design's own 5 % hard-gap criterion; lowering the field to suppress it narrows the window by the identical factor. Over a two-parameter feasibility map (five dome critical fields × two estimation routes), 9 of 10 combinations have no solution, and the single survivor clears the 0.5 mT remanent-field floor by only 5 %.

Consequences: (i) a two-vortex parity device runs at even vorticity; under a power-law (nodal) hybridisation `δE = Δ(ξ/L)^p` the parity splitting at 500 nm moves from 10.6 neV (gapped law) to 2.72–26.5 μeV for p = 2 to p = 1; the exponent p is a sensitivity scan, not established; (ii) the zero-field hard-gap check is passed by a clean nodal gap at 20 mK (`G(0)/G_N` = 0.92 %), so it cannot detect the failure it was meant to catch; (iii) the quantised-peak test is contaminated by a nodal background that exceeds its 7 % margin in 3 of 4 estimates.

The open theory window (Ising spin–orbit coupling from dichalcogenide proximity) was then evaluated. On the physical inter-valley pairing channel on the real Bistritzer–MacDonald bands, the Chern parity is even at every grid point, for both order parameters and every nematic director angle checked; the per-valley mechanism is genuinely active, and an intra-valley (pair-density-wave-type) channel at generic director angle goes odd at 13 grid points.

The question is cheap to close: `G(0)/G_N` versus perpendicular field in 0.5 mT steps through one point contact on the same mesa. A `√B` law kills the route; flat-then-activated reopens it.
